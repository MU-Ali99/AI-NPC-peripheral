# v0.4.1

The default model is now `qwen3:4b-instruct-2507-q4_K_M`. It produced more varied confrontations and recalled earlier player messages directly, while the regular Qwen 3 thinking model and Qwen 3.5 4B exhausted the response budget without usable dialogue on this CPU.

Every relationship state now includes a concrete acting direction. Neutral characters remain reserved, offended characters identify damaged trust and set boundaries, very negative characters treat hostility as a pattern, and hostile characters may refuse conversation. Positive stages progress from ordinary civility through familiarity, trust, and personal vulnerability.

NPCBridge rejects one-word dialogue and any exact reply found in recent history, then asks the model for a fresh response.

Packaged evaluation found correct sentiment and score movement in all tested cases. Hostile-stage differentiation improved substantially. Warm and very-close responses were still less differentiated than intended, which remains a known limitation of a 4B model on this machine.
