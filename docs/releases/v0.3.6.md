# v0.3.6 - Context-Aware Hostility

- Phrases about punching, killing, smashing, hurting, beating, or attacking an NPC are classified as hostile threats.
- Threats receive stronger friendship penalties than ordinary insults.
- Fallback dialogue now distinguishes threats, profanity, age insults, general insults, and established grudges.
- Linus no longer mentions age when the player's remark has nothing to do with age.
- Compliments such as “you have a kind face” are recognized explicitly.
- Direct hostility uses deterministic character-specific boundaries instead of relying on a small model to judge whether a threat is serious.
